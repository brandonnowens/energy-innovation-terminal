# Public Technical Deliverable & Award Outcome Report
## Award ID: ASST_NON_DEMS0000099_089
**Project Title:** BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) – DOMESTIC PRODUCTION OF LITHIUM CARBONATE FROM THE SMACKOVER FORMATION BRINES IN SOUTHERN ARKANSAS
