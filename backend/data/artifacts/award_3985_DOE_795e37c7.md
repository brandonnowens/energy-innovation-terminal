# Public Technical Deliverable & Award Outcome Report
## Award ID: ASST_NON_DEMS0000107_089
**Project Title:** BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) ENABLING THE SILICON BATTERY SUPPLY CHAIN BY BUILDING A ROBUST U.S. SILANE MANUFACTURING CAPACITY.
THE OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT IS TO REDUCE FOREIGN BATTERY SUPPLY CHAIN DEPENDENCE BY INSTALLING, COMMISSIONING, AND OPERATING A U.S.-BASED SILANE MANUFACTURING PLANT WITH 7,200 METRIC TON ANNUAL CAPACITY TO FEED PRODUCTION OF ADVANCED SILICON-BASED ANODE MATERIALS
