### Project Abstract & Objective
BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) - RAPID:  REALIZING ADVANCED PRODUCTION OF IRON-AIR BATTERIES FOR COMMERCIAL DEPLOYMENT
THIS PROJECT WILL PROCURE AND INSTALL FORM FACTORY 1, LLC'S (FF1) FIRST 15 GIGAWATT HOUR (GWH) SEMI-AUTOMATED IRON-AIR BATTERY MANUFACTURING LINE AT ITS NEW MANUFACTURING FACILITY.  THIS FACTORY WILL MAKE 100-HOUR IRON-AIR MULTI-DAY STORAGE ENERGY SYSTEMS THAT ENABLE THE GRID TO RUN COST-EFFECTIVELY AND RELIABLY ON CLEAN ELECTRICITY YEAR-ROUND.  FF1 WILL ACCOMPLISH THIS BY BUILDING A NEW SEMI-AUTOMATED MANUFACTURING LINE AND RAMPING TO AT LEAST 15 GWH/YR CAPACITY.
