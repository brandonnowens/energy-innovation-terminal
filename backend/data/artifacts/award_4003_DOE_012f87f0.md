# Public Technical Deliverable & Award Outcome Report

**Project Title:** BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) - LARGE SCALE, ENERGY EFFICIENT, DOMESTIC PRODUCTION OF HIGH-PERFORMANCE SYNTHETIC GRAPHITE ANODE MATERIAL FOR USE IN ELECTRIC VEHICLES AND ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS
