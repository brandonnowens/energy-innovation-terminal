BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) - COMMERCIAL SCALE CONTINUOUS HEAT TREATMENT (CHT) PROCESS FOR NEXT GENERATION SYNTHETIC GRAPHITE PRODUCTION.
