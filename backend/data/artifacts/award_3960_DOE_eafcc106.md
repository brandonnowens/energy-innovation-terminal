# Public Technical Deliverable & Award Outcome Report
## Award ID: ASST_NON_DEMS0000008_089
**Project Title:** BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) SOLVAY BATTERY-GRADE PVDF MANUFACTURING FACILITY.

THE OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT IS TO DESIGN, CONSTRUCT, AND OPERATE A NEW POLYVINYLIDENE DIFLUORIDE (PVDF) PRODUCTION FACILITY AT AN EXISTING RECIPIENT FACILITY.  THE PVDF PRODUCT WILL BE OF AN APPROPRIATE GRADE FOR THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) AND STATIONARY ENERGY STORAGE MARKETS.

### Project Abstract & Objective
BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE LAW (BIL) SOLVAY BATTERY-GRADE PVDF MANUFACTURING FACILITY.

THE OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT IS TO DESIGN, CONSTRUCT, AND OPERATE A NEW POLYVINYLIDENE DIFLUORIDE (PVDF) PRODUCTION FACILITY AT AN EXISTING RECIPIENT FACILITY.  THE PVDF PRODUCT WILL BE OF AN APPROPRIATE GRADE FOR THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE (EV) AND STATIONARY ENERGY STORAGE MARKETS.
